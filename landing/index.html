Common questions

Is this real customer data?

The included demo and private pilot use fictional records. A separate browser-only evaluator lets you test authorized, minimized CSV or Excel exports without uploading them. Private cloud processing of customer records remains gated.

Does ShiftClarity change invoices?

No. It does not edit source invoices or collect money. It shows differences, the evidence and your review decision. Any dollar figure is an estimate, not collected cash.

Can I use exports from my current systems?

Start with CSV or Excel exports from your current timekeeping, billing and approved-rate register. Test them locally with confirmed mappings and download a review report. Choose a values-only Excel worksheet or CSV UTF-8 file. The private synthetic pilot also reads XLSX. Preparation guidance covers Bullhorn, Avionté and VMS/statement workflows. Exact formats need confirmation; no native connection or vendor certification is implied. PDF/OCR import is not enabled.

What will I get back?

Potential underbilling and overbilling, the approved-work calculations and signed invoice allocations behind them, your review decisions and the records that could not be evaluated. Download Excel/CSV or print/save PDF. Differences are candidates for review, not recovered revenue or evidence of payment.

What do I need for the first review?

One closed period, detailed work and posted billing files with stable IDs, and the approved hourly-rate register with effective dates. Confirm source coverage and approval references. The evaluator guides field mappings and offers realistic fictional files with independent expected results.
