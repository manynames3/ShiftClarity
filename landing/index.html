Common questions

Is this real customer data?

No. The demo and private pilot use fictional records only. The pilot supports minimized synthetic CSV, Excel and normalized JSON imports. Real customer data remains gated.

Does ShiftClarity change invoices?

No. It does not edit source invoices or collect money. It shows differences, the evidence and your review decision. Any dollar figure is an estimate, not collected cash.

Can I connect my systems?

The private pilot accepts CSV or Excel exports with confirmed column and value mappings. It has no live system connectors or PDF/OCR import. Try the fictional demo, or use an assigned pilot account to test the spreadsheet workflow.
