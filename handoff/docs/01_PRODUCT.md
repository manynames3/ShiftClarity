# 01 · Product and commercial brief

## Identity
**Name:** ShiftClarity (working brand; domain/trademark clearance is not established).
**Endorsement:** A Supreme AI Ventures product.
**Tagline:** Every shift. Clearly aligned.
**Descriptor:** Contract-to-invoice verification for healthcare staffing.
**Promise:** Help teams keep approved agreements, completed work and invoices aligned, with evidence for every review decision.

Do not use RevenueDesk, ShiftVerity, revenue recovery, collections AI, automated auditor, guaranteed accuracy, "every detail verified", or an industry-wide superiority claim as positioning.

## Who it serves
The initial buyer is the owner or billing/operations leader at a healthcare staffing agency serving multiple facilities under changing hourly agreements. The day-to-day user is a billing specialist who currently compares contracts, approved work and billing outputs. An operations coordinator may resolve missing approvals. William / Assist Healthcare Staffing is a prospective first client, not a confirmed customer.

The first demo is privately personalized as **Assist Healthcare · Sample workspace**. Use fictional facilities and pseudonymous workers. Do not reproduce Assist's logo, claim its endorsement, or publish this customer-specific workspace without approval.

## The primary problem
A system may generate internally consistent invoices from a configured rate that does not match an approved agreement or amendment. We want to verify the agreement-to-work-to-invoice connection independently and show clear evidence when it differs.

The initial focus is **effective-dated contract-change verification**. Two adjacent demo cases make the benefit concrete: eligible work not represented in the supplied completed billing extract, and an approval blocking review. These are not evidence that Assist has these problems.

## The differentiation hypothesis
A narrow, independent evidence workflow may reduce manual verification or identify discrepancies that the existing process misses. Scheduling, rate cards, invoices, versioning and AR automation are not unique capabilities. Determine whether the hypothesis is true using the actual tools and workflows already available to Assist. Prefer configuration help to a redundant product if existing functionality solves the problem.

## In scope for the synthetic demo
Local hourly staffing, USD, two fictional facilities, approved fixed rates, explicit effective dates, complete posted-invoice extracts, straightforward explicit references, one many-to-one invoice example, one credit/rebill chain, two financial findings and two nonfinancial coverage gaps. Only the approved synthetic formats are importable. The demo clock is fixed at 2026-10-12 17:00 America/New_York.

## Out of scope
Scheduling, credentialing, recruiting, payroll calculation, travel/stipends, insurance claims, Medicaid/EVV, home-care billing, state licensing/procurement, margin analytics, collection automation, predictive dashboards, LLM contract extraction, arbitrary integrations, live messaging and changing source-system records.

Overtime, callback, holiday and other unsupported components are visible coverage gaps, not quietly assumed to use the ordinary rate. Do not build a general rules language or infer legal contract precedence.

## Buyer benefit and measurement
Accuracy includes detecting overbilling as well as underbilling. Time savings must include export preparation, mapping, rule approval and investigation. Confirmed discrepancies, approved adjustments, invoiced adjustments and cash collection are different outcomes. Without payment/remittance evidence, there is no collected/recovered amount metric.

The owner view prioritizes coverage, decisions and unresolved blockers, not an enticing cash total. Money is evidence inside a finding, not the product's personality.

## Commercial assumptions (PRIVATE, not public UI)
The prior discussion proposed a free synthetic walkthrough, a **$2,500 fixed-scope diagnostic**, and **$995/month** for a limited supported ongoing workflow after a successful pilot. These are unvalidated prices. A $1,995 expansion tier is deferred until its limits and value are known.

Do not promise unlimited facilities, integrations, support or custom development at these prices. Implementation scope and cost need a written quote after the diagnostic. Any credit of the diagnostic fee toward implementation must appear in that quote; it is not a free-implementation promise. No published pricing, payment processor or subscription billing in this build.

## Required discovery before real records
Identify the actual timekeeping, rate-management and invoice systems and licensed modules; inspect authorized sample export layouts; locate a recent concrete rate-change problem; determine the billing cutoff/calendar and source completeness rules; confirm document precedence with an authorized reviewer; understand William's existing product and ownership expectations; agree data-use terms, evaluation criteria and implementation scope.

A useful paid diagnostic is a legitimate client engagement even when it does not justify a recurring SaaS. Do not confuse a polished demo with product-market fit.
